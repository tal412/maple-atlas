Get Maple Atlas Companion

Run the free companion app on the computer with the game. Scan its QR once with your phone and it stays paired: your dot follows you live, with directions.

Download for Windows Download for Mac
  1. Windows: run MapleAtlasCompanion-Setup.exe. If SmartScreen appears, choose More info → Run anyway, and allow private networks when the firewall asks.
  2. Mac: open the downloaded file and drag the app to Applications. If macOS blocks it the first time, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  3. Open Maple Atlas on your phone and scan the QR the app shows. Only once.

    Connection help & offline use

    Maple Atlas Companion runs on Windows and macOS. Scan its QR once: the phone remembers the computer and reconnects by itself, or with one tap on Reconnect.

    Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi network. A phone on cellular data cannot connect. Guest Wi-Fi, VPNs and firewalls can also block it; on Windows, allow the app on private networks when the firewall asks.

    Nothing is sent to the internet: the phone and computer talk directly, and only the map you're on, your position and the quests in your Quest Helper travel between them.

    The first download needs internet. Saved map data can then run locally. Market updates still need internet.

    Your Classic World map, live on your phone

    Maple Atlas follows your game. The free companion app reads your in-game minimap, and your phone shows exactly where you are, with directions through every recorded portal to where you want to go.

    How it works

    1. Run the companion on your computer

      Pick the game window. It reads only the minimap, and screen images never leave your computer.

    2. Scan once with your phone

      Point your phone's camera at the QR. After that it reconnects by itself, even after restarts.

    3. Follow live directions

      Your dot moves on the map as you play, with hints like “Head right, about one screen” toward the next portal.
